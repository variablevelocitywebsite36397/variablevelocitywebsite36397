About the Team

Built for the turns, not just the straightaway.

Variable Velocity is FIRST Tech Challenge Team 36397 — a student-run team that treats every match like a system with changing conditions. We design robots that hold precision whether they're moving fast, slow, or somewhere in between.

Our process is iterative and CAD-first: prototype, test on the field, measure what actually happened, and redesign. We value the data as much as the result, and we document our reasoning so the next build season starts smarter than the last.

Off the field, we bring that same engineering mindset to our community — running workshops, mentoring younger teams, and making STEM feel approachable to students who haven't found their way into it yet.

Mission & Values

What drives the build.

Three principles steer every design decision, every mentorship session, and every match strategy we run.

PRECISION

Control Over Chaos

Speed means nothing without control. We engineer for consistency across every condition the game throws at us.

ITERATION

Test, Measure, Rebuild

Every prototype is a hypothesis. We track what fails as carefully as what works, and we're not precious about starting over.

ACCESS

Open the Track

We mentor newer teams and run outreach because the fastest way to grow the sport is to bring more people onto the field.
